    Off Track

    Driver profile

    I got into engineering the way a lot of people get into cars: by wanting to know what was happening underneath. Formula 1 is the version of that obsession I never grew out of, because it's a sport where the engineering is the story. A tenth of a second is a design decision someone made months earlier.

    That's roughly why I like embedded systems. A race car and an avionics bay have the same shape of problem: a hard latency budget, sensors that lie to you, and a system that has to keep working when a subsystem doesn't. The ESP32 radar and the RoboMaster vision pipeline are both small versions of that.

    Longer term, I want to go one layer deeper, into chip design and computer architecture. Logic Design this term is the first step on that road.

    When I'm not building, I'm usually in Forza Horizon or Forza Motorsport tuning something that doesn't need tuning, out on the bike on a trail, or watching a race.
